// 01 — About Me

I Build Systems That Work in the Real World

I am an Electronic and Computer Engineer who builds complete systems—from hardware to software and everything in between. My work spans embedded firmware on ESP32 microcontrollers, payment-integrated IoT devices, Django and Node.js backends, Flutter mobile apps, and relational database design.

I have built systems that process real M-Pesa transactions, track live delivery riders, simulate phishing attacks for security awareness training, and monitor electricity consumption anomalies in real time. I approach every engineering challenge as a systems thinker—understanding how hardware, software, data, and people connect to create solutions that actually solve problems.

I hold a BSc. in Electronic and Computer Engineering—Second Class Honours (Upper Division) from JKUAT, awarded July 2026. Through attachments at Kenya Power and Lighting Company, Fedha Electrics, and JHUB Africa, I have worked across electrical infrastructure, electronics design, and agile software delivery.
